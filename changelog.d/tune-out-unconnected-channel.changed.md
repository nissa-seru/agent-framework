- `tune_out` and `focus` mode `check` mark an entry whose channel isn't registered `connected: false`, with `lastSeenLabel`, the latest label its id was seen with on any server, where there is one.
- An id that two tune-outs share, once neither's channel is registered, is refused by `tune_out`'s cancel and peek and by `cancel_tuneout`, saying how to name each alone. A name a registered channel answers to still means that channel.
- The refusal of a cancel that names no tune-out says "no channel … is tuned out" where it said "no connected channel", lists every tune-out, and marks those whose channel isn't connected now. A channel that isn't registered, whose id another such tune-out shares, is shown by its server-qualified name there, in a cancel's answer and in the backlog's notice.
