- The Discord module no longer edits a turn's thoughts into a message posted before an attempt to post that was refused or threw. It edits a tool turn's thoughts, as a spoiler, into the message the latest `send`, `reply` or speech posted. When the client threw, or the rate limit or a missing reply context refused the attempt, the message from before it stayed in place, so the thoughts were edited into a message people may already have read. A `dm`, which posts but can't take the thoughts, did the same. Each of these now forgets the earlier message when it starts. The cost is that a turn which posts and then makes such an attempt, or one whose post another agent's attempt follows, now drops its thoughts rather than editing them into its own post.
