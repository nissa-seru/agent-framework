- A turn whose stream fails, and whose error-policy retry then fails before it has a stream of its own (its compile refused, or its call failing as its stream is made), no longer leaves the agent counted as streaming. Before, `runUntilIdle()` never settled after such a turn, and a closing conversation agent whose turn ended that way was never disposed. A resident's next turn cleared it, so a resident that kept getting messages recovered by itself.
