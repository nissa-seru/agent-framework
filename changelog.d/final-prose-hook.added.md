- `Module.onFinalProse(agentName, parts)`: once per logical turn end, after the turn's final routing is decided and before its receipt where it has one, how each run or envelope of the turn's closing words ended: `routed`, `private`, `sent` (with `where` and `delivered`), `sent-unknown`, `no-destination`, `disabled`, `silent` or `no-registry`, with the words only where a host may hand them on (`routed`, `disabled`, `no-registry`). A notice the framework doesn't await; not called for a turn that fails or is stopped. Before, a host relaying an agent's reply could go only by the round's shape, so words the framework held went out as the reply.
- `FinalProsePart`: the type of each part `onFinalProse` is told.
- `ModuleRegistry.notifyFinalProse(agentName, parts)`: tells each module, with its own copy of the parts; a module that throws or rejects is logged and changes nothing.
