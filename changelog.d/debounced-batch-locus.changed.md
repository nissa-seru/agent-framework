- A debounced gate batch in which no message addressed the agent, and whose messages the host could place all came from one channel, now carries that channel as its route (`WakeProvenance.routeChannelId`), and the agent's reply goes there under either `speech.locus`, as a direct wake for one of those messages does. Before, such a batch set no route: under `'delivery'` its plain speech went nowhere, and under `'latest-inbound'` it took the shared fallback. A batch from several channels still sets no route, and names them in the new `WakeProvenance.batchChannels`. A `chat:system` notice in a batch no longer decides the route, the `addressed` flag or the channel the wake names, as for a direct wake. The wake of a batch of notices alone carries only the author of its newest notice that has one.
