- A channel tune-out whose channel isn't registered any more, because its server removed the channel (as a connector does when a channel is deleted or a thread archives) or the server itself went away, can be seen and ended again, as a tune-out with no duration lasts until cancelled. `tune_out` and `focus` mode `check` list it, `peek` shows what it holds, and `tune_out` mode `cancel` ends it and delivers its backlog. Each takes the channel's id with `serverId`, its server-qualified name, or its id alone where no registered channel answers to the id and no other such tune-out shares it. The subconscious's `cancel_tuneout` takes its id or its server-qualified name the same way, and inside a turn whose own channel its server withdrew, that channel's id. Before, `check` didn't list such a tune-out, `tune_out`'s cancel and peek answered "unknown channel", and `cancel_tuneout` without a `serverId`, which its schema doesn't declare, refused it as not tuned out. So a tune-out with no duration could hold its backlog until the channel registered again, which a deleted channel never does.
